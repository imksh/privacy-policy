1

Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the Android application GSTHisab – GST Invoice Maker (referred to as "GSTHisab", "the App", or "we"), distributed via the Google Play Store and developed by [Developer / Company Name].

GSTHisab is an offline-first GST invoice generation, tax estimation, and professional billing utility built specifically for Indian shopkeepers, small retail businesses, traders, MSMEs, freelancers, and individuals.

This policy establishes our commitment to compliance under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023) of India, the Information Technology Act, 2000 (along with applicable intermediary guidelines), and the Google Play Developer Program Policies on User Data.

2

Fundamental Architectural Principle: 100% Offline-First

GSTHisab is built with an offline-first foundation. The application operates without remote server dependencies. All core functionalities—including product catalog management, customer records, tax computation (CGST, SGST, IGST), and PDF compilation—are performed directly inside the application execution environment on your Android device.

Zero Remote Database Transmission: We do not operate, lease, or connect to any remote cloud databases (such as AWS, Firebase, Supabase, or private backend servers) to store or mirror your business records.

3

Personal Information Handled

GSTHisab does not require account creation, phone number verification, or email registration. Users can use the application immediately upon installation.

To personalize your GST bills, you may voluntarily input into the Settings screen:

  • Business Owner / Authorized Signatory Name: Printed on invoice declarations and signatures.
  • Business Contact Phone Number & Email: Displayed on bills for customer communication.
  • Digital Signature / Logo Image: Stored locally if you photograph or select an image file to embed on bills.

This information is saved solely in your device's local database and is never transmitted off your phone.

4

Business, Commercial & Tax Information (GSTIN)

To generate statutory Indian Goods and Services Tax invoices complying with Central GST and State GST rules, you can maintain:

  • Business Profile: Trade name, legal business name, GSTIN (15-digit GST number), State (Place of Supply), registered business address, and PAN.
  • Bank Settlement Details: Bank Name, Account Holder Name, Account Number, and IFSC Code (printed on invoice footers for your buyers' bank transfers).
  • Customer Directory: Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, and buyer GSTINs.
  • Product / Service Catalog: Item names, HSN / SAC codes, unit prices, and GST rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%).

Government / Tax Authority Clarification: GSTHisab does NOT automatically upload or report your GSTIN, sales transactions, or turnover to the GST Portal (GSTN), CBIC, or state tax departments.

5

Financial and Transaction-Related Information

The Application calculates taxable values, applicable CGST, SGST, IGST totals, and amount in words for each generated invoice.

Payment Disclaimer: GSTHisab is not a payment gateway, payment aggregator, or financial wallet. We do not collect, process, or store credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs. Any payment instructions printed on bills (such as bank details or UPI handles) are entered by you manually.

6

Device Information, Diagnostics and Analytics

We do not collect your Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, hardware serial numbers, or contacts. We do not incorporate background analytical tracking SDKs or behavioral telemetry beacons.

7

Android Device Permissions and Why They Are Required

In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Permission policies, GSTHisab requests only the minimum runtime permissions necessary to deliver user-requested features:

  • android.permission.CAMERA (Optional): Allows taking a photograph of a physical business logo or signature to display on invoice headers. Captured images remain strictly on the local device.
  • Media & Photo Gallery (Optional): Allows picking an existing logo image or signature from device storage.
  • Scoped Storage / PDF Sharing (Storage Access Framework): Enables compiling, saving, and exporting PDF invoices to device storage and sharing them via the native Android share sheet.

You can revoke these permissions at any time via Android device settings without disabling core invoice calculations.

8

Data Storage, Isolation and Local Protection

All records are stored in a local SQLite database located in Android's private internal application sandbox (/data/data/com.gsthisab.invoicemaker/databases/). Under Android's Linux UID isolation, other applications installed on your phone cannot access or read your GSTHisab database.

Zero Data Sharing, Sale or Commercialization

We do not sell, rent, monetize, trade, or share your business, personal, or financial records with any third party, broker, or advertising network. Because data never leaves your physical phone, we have zero access to your database.

12

Data Retention and Deletion Procedures

You have complete control over data retention:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual bills, customers, or items at any time within the app.
  • Permanent Device Wipe: Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage via Android Settings > Apps > GSTHisab > Storage > Clear Data immediately and permanently deletes 100% of the SQLite database and all saved files.

User Rights under Indian DPDPA 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), Indian users have:

  • Right to Access (Section 11): Unrestricted access to all stored information within the application.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): The right to update, modify, or permanently erase records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Right to seek redressal from our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): Right to designate a nominee to handle data rights.
